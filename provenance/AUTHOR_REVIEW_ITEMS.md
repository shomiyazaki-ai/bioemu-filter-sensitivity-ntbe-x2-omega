# Author review before any release

Review the two versioned PDFs, especially Methods signs/settings and the limits on operational estimates and L2 evidence. Confirm carried-forward affiliation, funding, competing interests, AI disclosure and rights scope. Confirm the title and proposed final version 1.1.0, and decide whether the candidate is suitable for publication. A new DOI, public commit and tag can only be recorded after the separately approved release operation actually creates them.

The P3 judgment concerns readiness for this review. It does not constitute publication authorization, peer review, or proof of general scientific novelty. No pending author-review item requires additional research under this production scope.
